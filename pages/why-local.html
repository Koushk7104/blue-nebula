Local needs to earn
its place in the workflow.

Hardware sets a boundary

Memory, compute, model choice, and workload affect what a local runtime can support. The initial product should qualify a narrow complete workflow before broadening its scope.

Local doesn't prove correctness

A locally generated change still needs review and checks. Evidence, sources, and explicit actions remain part of the design regardless of where a model runs.

Training is a separate workflow

Project memory updates would not silently retrain the model. Later adapter experiments need separate consent, held-out evaluation, and a reversible promotion path.
